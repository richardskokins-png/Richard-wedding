Open the fictional Google Sheets ledger →

The Reference sales and Reference expenses tabs contain the fixed assignment examples. Sales and Expenses are reserved for future live tests. Changes in this browser’s Reference mode do not update Sheets.

1. Enter a fictional test transaction

Choose Richard in the role menu, then New transaction. The example button fills a unique reference, fictional customer, Project A, €100, and a 50% / 30% / 20% commission split. Press Save sale for approval.

Expected: the sale is pending. Approved income and commissions stay unchanged until a manager approves it.

For an expense, choose Kevin and enter €10 with Company overhead. It reduces company result immediately.

2. Use the manager test controls

  1. Choose Svetlana de Monte Carlo from the role menu.
  2. Open Decisions, find your unique reference, and approve the sale. You may change the split as long as it totals 100%.
  3. Open Records to compare the original proposal with the final decision.
  4. Open Overview: approving the €100 sale adds €100 income, €10 commission expense, and €90 company result. At 50/30/20, commissions increase by €5, €3, and €2.
  5. The €10 overhead expense reduces company result by €10. Together the two sample entries increase it by €80.

These role choices are public homework controls, not production account authentication. Use fictional information only.

3. Link your Telegram account

  1. When the bot is connected, choose Svetlana → Manager setup.
  2. Choose the fictional employee Richard and press Create my Telegram link.
  3. Open that link in your own private Telegram chat and press Start. It expires after 10 minutes and can be used once.
  4. The bot confirms the fictional role. You do not need to share account IDs, passwords, or bot tokens.

Send the command below using a fresh reference. Wait for the bot’s confirmation, then approve it as Svetlana on the website. The same reference should appear once in the live Sales tab.

4. Check delivery and repeat the test
With live services configured, Records shows Sheets and Telegram delivery status. Svetlana can use Retry Sheets or Retry Telegram on a failed delivery. Retries keep the transaction reference and accounting totals unchanged.
